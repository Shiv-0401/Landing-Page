F1 Race Outcome Prediction

A machine-learning pipeline designed to predict Formula 1 race outcomes while analyzing model robustness and explainability.

  • Processed 26,760 historical race entries.
  • Combined K-Means clustering with XGBoost, Random Forest, and Logistic Regression.
  • Used strict temporal splits, SHAP analysis, and ablation testing to reduce leakage risk.
  • Reached 5-fold cross-validation AUC of 0.9038 +/- 0.0052.
